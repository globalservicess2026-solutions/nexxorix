Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about our services, pricing, and how we work. If yours isn't here, just ask.

What services does Nexxorix offer?

We offer SEO services, Google Ads, Meta Ads, website development, lead generation websites, e-commerce websites, chatbot automation, AI calling agents, and CRM integration. You can use one service or combine several.

How much do your services cost?

Pricing depends on your requirements, so we quote each project individually. Get in touch with a short description of what you need and our team will come back to you.

Do you guarantee Google rankings, leads, or sales?

No. Rankings, leads, and sales depend on many factors, including competition, budget, and your market, and nobody can honestly guarantee them. We focus on the work that improves your chances and we're upfront about what we're doing.

How long does it take to build a website?

It depends on the number of pages, the content, and the features you need. Once we understand your requirements, the team can give you a realistic estimate.

Do you build WordPress websites?

Yes. WordPress is one of the options we work with for business websites, alongside custom-built sites and landing pages.

What is the difference between SEO and Google Ads?

SEO works on improving your website so it appears more often in unpaid search results, and it tends to build over time. Google Ads are paid, so your business can appear in search results while a campaign is running. Many businesses use both.

Do you run Facebook and Instagram ads?

Yes. Our Meta Ads service covers Facebook and Instagram advertising, including lead generation campaigns, audience targeting, and retargeting.

Can you build an online store with payments?

Yes. Our e-commerce websites include product and catalog setup, shopping cart and checkout, and payment gateway integration.

What is an AI calling agent?

It's a voice assistant that can handle phone calls, ask a few basic questions, and pass the details to your team. The exact setup depends on your business and is agreed with you beforehand.

Can you connect my website and ads to our CRM?

Yes, that's what our CRM integration service is for. Tell us which CRM you use and we'll confirm what's possible.

Can I speak to a real person instead of the chatbot?

Absolutely. Tell the chat assistant you'd like to talk to the team, use the contact page, call or message us on WhatsApp at +1 810 278 9121.

How do I get started?

Use the Get Started page or contact us directly. Tell us about your business and your goal, and we'll suggest a way forward.

Still have a question?

Ask our team directly and we'll get back to you.
